Frequently asked questions

How do I record a run?

Open the Track tab, wait for the GPS signal to lock, and press start. PACE records your route, distance, pace, and time. Press and hold the finish button to save the run to your history and feed.

How do I import runs from Apple Health?

When you first open PACE it asks for permission to read your workouts from Apple Health. If you allowed it, runs recorded by your Apple Watch or other apps are imported automatically. You can change the permission any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → PACE Running Club. PACE never writes any data back to Apple Health.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the login screen tap Forgot password and enter your email. We’ll send you a code to set a new password. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that same button to sign back in.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the … menu on any post, comment, or profile to report content or block a user. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and review every report — typically within 24 hours — removing content and suspending accounts that break our Terms of Service.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently deletes your account and associated personal data. If you have any trouble, email us and we’ll take care of it.

My runs aren’t tracking accurately

Accurate GPS needs location access set to Always or While Using with Precise Location on (iOS Settings → PACE Running Club → Location). Give the signal a few seconds to lock before starting, and keep the app allowed to run in the background during a recording. Still seeing issues? Email us with the date and time of the run.
